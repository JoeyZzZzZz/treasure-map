# License FAQ

## Why AGPL-3.0 instead of MIT?

We chose AGPL-3.0 to keep Treasure Map sustainable as a long-term 
open-source project while preventing closed-source commercial use 
without contribution back.

## Can I use Treasure Map for my own research?

Yes, freely. AGPL-3.0 places no restrictions on individual use, 
academic research, vulnerability research, blog posts, conference 
talks, or any non-service usage.

## Can I use Treasure Map in my company's internal security work?

Yes, freely, as long as you don't expose Treasure Map's modified 
version as a service to others outside your company.

## Can I run Treasure Map as a SaaS for my customers?

If you modify Treasure Map and run the modified version as a service, 
AGPL-3.0 requires you to open-source your modifications under the 
same license. For commercial licensing without this requirement, 
please contact the maintainer.

## Can I fork Treasure Map?

Yes, under the terms of AGPL-3.0. Your fork must also be AGPL-3.0.

## What about contributors?

We require contributors to sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). 
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
